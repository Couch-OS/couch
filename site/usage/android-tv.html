ANDROID & GOOGLE TV

Control Android and Google TV

Pair Android TV once in Connections, add it to a room, then select its row and press OK. Navigation and media keys go straight to that TV.

Physical buttons

Default behavior. An active activity can override individual mappings.

Physical button mappings for Android & Google TV
ButtonWhat it does
D-pad and OKNavigate the TV and select its focused item.
BackSend Back to Android TV. A short press stays in the device screen.
Home and MenuOpen the TV home screen or send its menu key.
Volume + / − and MuteAdjust TV volume or toggle mute. What changes depends on the TV’s audio setup.
Channel + / −Send channel-up or channel-down to the active TV app.
PowerSend power-off when the TV reports it is on, or power-on when it reports standby. If its state is unknown, Couch asks you to retry rather than guessing.
Hold BackHold for about 0.6 seconds to return to your previous Couch device list.
Color buttonsNot supported by this Android TV client yet; use another supported activity mapping if needed.

Pair the TV

In the web UI, add an Android / Google TV connection. Find TVs on this network, choose yours, request the on-screen code, and verify it. Then add the connection as a device in a room. The TV must have Android TV Remote Service enabled; ADB is not required.

Add favorite apps

In that connection’s settings, add an app name and its launch link under App shortcuts. Reorder the entries and select Save app shortcuts. Open Apps on the remote to launch one. This is a configured list, not installed-app discovery: a link only opens if an installed TV app handles it.

Use touch for playback

Play, pause, rewind, fast-forward, previous, next and stop use Android media keys. Support depends on the active app. Couch reads standard Cast media status when available to show the title, app, playback state and artwork. The timeline shows elapsed time and duration; it is read-only. Live streams show Live, and unknown durations are left unset. Some apps expose limited metadata or none, so artwork and playback details may be absent. Chapter discovery is not supported.

When a connection drops

Check that the TV is awake and reachable, then use Reconnect. Stale navigation presses are discarded instead of replayed after a slow reconnect. In an activity, custom button mappings can send individual keys to other devices.
