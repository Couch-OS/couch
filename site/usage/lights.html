LIGHTS & SCENES

Dim a light without opening another screen.

Highlight a light or a Hue room in the device list. Its physical controls are ready immediately; brightness and scene changes appear in a temporary card.

Physical buttons

Default behavior. An active activity can override individual mappings.

Physical button mappings for Lights & scenes
ButtonWhat it does
D-pad ↑ / ↓Highlight the light or Hue room you want to control.
OKToggle the selected light or Hue room on or off.
Volume + / −Adjust brightness in five-percentage-point steps when the selected item supports dimming.
Channel + / −Cycle forward or backward through this room’s configured scenes.
BackLeave the room. Brightness and scene feedback clear when you navigate away.

Scenes belong to rooms

In the web UI, edit a room to add Hue lights, Hue rooms and scenes from its bridge. The Scenes button at the bottom of the device list opens that room’s scene choices. Select a scene and press OK to recall it.

Read the status at a glance

Light icons brighten when on and fade when off. Room icons reflect the reported state of their devices. A Hue room is a bridge group, so its brightness control affects the lights in that group.

These are the default list controls. Activity button mappings can route volume and other keys to a different device while that activity is active.
