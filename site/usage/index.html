USING COUCH

A screen for context.
Buttons for control.

Learn what the remote’s buttons do as you move from rooms to lights and media. Start with the default controls, then customize them for your activities.

Rooms & navigation

The home screen collects your rooms, activities and scenes in one area. Open a room to see its devices in a single list.

Read guide

Lights & scenes

Highlight a light or a Hue room in the device list. Its physical controls are ready immediately; brightness and scene changes appear in a temporary card.

Read guide

Blinds & thermostats

Use Volume to position blinds or adjust a thermostat target. OK toggles a blind or opens the thermostat’s dedicated view.

Read guide

Kodi & activities

Open a Kodi device from a room to take control immediately. The playback screen uses its artwork, title, timeline and available media options.

Read guide

Android & Google TV

Pair Android TV once in Connections, add it to a room, then select its row and press OK. Navigation and media keys go straight to that TV.

Read guide

LG webOS TVs

Pair an LG webOS connection and add it to a room. Select the TV on the remote to use physical navigation, power, volume and color buttons immediately.

Read guide

Apple TV

Pair with an on-screen PIN, control Apple TV with physical buttons, and open its installed apps. Companion support is experimental.

Read guide

Infrared devices

Attach IR commands to an existing device or add an IR-only device.

Read guide

These guides track current development, so an older installation may look different. Try the interactive preview or browse the source.
